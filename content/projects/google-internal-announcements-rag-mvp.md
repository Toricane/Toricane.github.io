## Overview

The challenge asked how Google’s sales teams could keep up with product announcements that matter to their work. The MVP combines a personalized announcement view with a way to ask follow-up questions against the underlying documentation.

## Role and constraints

I designed and built the Gemini-powered MVP through The Knowledge Society’s Google Canada challenge.

## Process

Users select a role and region to receive relevant product updates. A retrieval-augmented chat interface then provides document-grounded responses to questions about those changes.

## Result

Google Canada ranked the solution first out of more than 100 submissions. I presented the MVP to a judging panel that included the Global Head of Partnerships.
