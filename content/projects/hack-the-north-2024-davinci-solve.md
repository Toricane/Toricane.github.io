## Overview

Leonardo Zhou and I built an interview-practice prototype for explaining the reasoning behind a programming solution aloud, rather than only completing a coding exercise.

## Process

We first built a console version, then wrapped it with Gradio. Groq and OpenAI provide speech and language-model functions, while Leetscrape and Beautiful Soup retrieve and format practice problems.

## Result

Completed the localhost MVP at our first hackathon. Separately, we placed second in MASV’s 30-minute file-upload lightning challenge at the same event.
